# Tax & Audit Plugin

**NOT TAX ADVICE.**

This plugin helps small business owners and real estate investors:
- Estimate quarterly tax payments
- Run cost segregation analyses on RE properties
- Evaluate entity structure options (LLC / S-Corp / C-Corp)
- Prepare for an IRS audit
- Find missed deductions by scanning QuickBooks Online transactions

It does NOT replace a CPA. Every calculation it produces is a model based on the inputs you provide and on publicly known tax rules. Tax law is complex, changes frequently, and varies by state. Before you file anything, **confirm with a licensed CPA or enrolled agent**.

The skills are designed to:
- Save you the slow work of organizing data
- Help you ask your CPA better-informed questions
- Spot opportunities your CPA might not flag if you haven't surfaced them

Use accordingly.
